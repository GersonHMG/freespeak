Progress panel

How a learner's Wrong / Partial / Perfect results improve day by day. Tap any day in a panel to see its breakdown.

Sample data, not real practice history. Score = Perfect 100, Partial 50, Wrong 0, averaged over every answer in the period, so a busy day counts for more than a light one. Colours use the app's 80 / 60 score-band cutoffs. Days with fewer than 5 answers are drawn faded.
